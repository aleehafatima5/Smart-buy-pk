Safe shopping tips

A few quick habits that protect your money when you buy online.

Spot the red flags

🏷️

Price too good to be true

If one seller is far cheaper than everyone else, the item may be fake, used, or a scam.

⭐

Fake reviews

Many 5-star reviews posted on the same day, with similar words, are a bad sign. Read the 3-star reviews too.

📸

Social media sellers

Check how long the page has existed, real customer comments, and whether they offer cash on delivery.

📱

Phones and PTA

Ask if a phone is PTA approved and check the box, IMEI and warranty before you pay.

✈️

International orders

Delivery can take weeks and customs charges may be added. Include them in the total price.

🔁

Return policy

Read how many days you have to return an item and who pays for the return delivery.
