Common questions

Does the site show live prices and ratings?

No. Stores like Daraz and social media sites do not allow other websites to read their data. So we show approximate price bands and give you live search links. You can type the prices you see into the "Compare live prices yourself" tool and it scores the stores for you.

How do you decide the best, cheapest and most trusted store?

It comes from how each type of product is usually sold in Pakistan. Phones are easy to compare on PriceOye, original beauty brands are safest from specialist stores, and used items are cheapest on OLX. Always check the live price before paying.

Is the health and beauty advice medical advice?

No. It is general information. Ask a doctor or pharmacist if you have a skin or health condition.

What if my product is not covered?

You still get a general buying guide, store links and review links. Try a simpler word, like "shampoo" instead of a long product name.

Is my data saved?

Only your recent searches and your light or dark theme are saved, inside your own browser.

Safe shopping tips

A few quick habits that protect your money when you buy online.

Spot the red flags

🏷️

Price too good to be true

If one seller is far cheaper than everyone else, the item may be fake, used, or a scam.

⭐

Fake reviews

Many 5-star reviews posted on the same day, with similar words, are a bad sign. Read the 3-star reviews too.

📸

Social media sellers

Check how long the page has existed, real customer comments, and whether they offer cash on delivery.

📱

Phones and PTA

Ask if a phone is PTA approved and check the box, IMEI and warranty before you pay.

✈️

International orders

Delivery can take weeks and customs charges may be added. Include them in the total price.

🔁

Return policy

Read how many days you have to return an item and who pays for the return delivery.
