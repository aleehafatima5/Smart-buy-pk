How the score is calculated

Every store gets a score out of 100.

Score formula

The score mixes price and quality:

Price part = lowest price you entered ÷ this store's price.

Quality part = rating ÷ 5, reduced a little when a store has few reviews. About 300 reviews gives full trust.

Verdict rules

Good buy ✅ Best store has rating 4.2 or more, at least 50 reviews, no serious warning, and is within your budget.

Not recommended ❌ Rating below 3.5, or the price is more than 20% over your budget.

Think first Everything in between.

Warnings we show

Very few reviews, a price far below the others (possible fake), above budget, low rating, and international delivery.

Common questions

Do I have to type the prices myself?

Yes. Each store sets its own prices and they change often, so you open the store, read the price, and type it in. SmartBuy PK then does the comparing and scoring for you.

Why are Pakistani stores shown first?

Local stores usually deliver faster, accept cash on delivery, and have simpler returns. International stores are listed after them with a "Check delivery" label.

What does the product picture do?

It is shown on your result card so you remember what you are checking. It stays on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Is my data saved?

Only your recent product names and your light or dark theme choice are saved, inside your own browser.
